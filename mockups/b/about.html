About

I shoot from the saddle.

I’m Greg Kabulski, a London-based photographer, and cycling is where my two passions meet. Most weekends you’ll find me somewhere between Regent’s Park, Richmond and the Lee Valley — riding the chaingangs, standing on the corners of a crit, or ankle-deep in cyclocross mud.

What sets my work apart is that I don’t wait at the roadside. I ride with the group and shoot on the move, camera in one hand and bars in the other, so I can get inside the peloton — the shoulder-to-shoulder tension of a bunch, the grimace on a climb, the light breaking through the trees on a morning loop. Being a rider myself means I know where the action will happen before it does.

London’s cycling community is the heart of it: the clubs, the café stops, the race organisers and the friends who turn up at 7am in the rain. I want to capture not just the speed and the drama of the sport, but its beauty and the people who make it.

Away from the bike I photograph streets, cities, landscapes, portraits and wildlife — the same eye for timing and light, just at a slower pace.
